# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include showing
empathy and kindness, being respectful of differing opinions, giving and gracefully
accepting constructive feedback, and focusing on what is best for the community.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, trolling, insulting or derogatory
comments, publishing others' private information, and other conduct which could
reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
to the maintainers at **conduct@switchboard.co**. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly.

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant][homepage], version 2.1.
